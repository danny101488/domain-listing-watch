    What this can and can't see: the sweeps read each name's Dynadot listing (official API) and its public nameservers. A sale through Atom, Spaceship or a private deal may not show until the listing comes down or the nameservers move, and a listing can come down for reasons other than a sale. Confirm anything flagged with Jake. Atom and Spaceship's own marketplace prices are not re-checked; the 27 Spaceship-registered names are watched through their Dynadot listings. Updated automatically every 6 hours by GitHub Actions using Dynadot's official API and public DNS. The radar bearing, run ID and Gini index are presentation: they don't add information beyond the prices and statuses.
